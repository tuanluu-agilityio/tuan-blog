Hi, I am Tuan. This is the first post on this blog.

I wanted a small, quiet place to keep notes on what I learn about programming, about work and, now and then, about everyday life.

## What I will write about

Short notes, written slowly and edited with care. No chasing trends, no ads, no tracking of readers.

## Languages

The blog is in Vietnamese and English. When a post has a translation, a language link appears at the top of the page.

Thanks for stopping by.
